Team Mission

We build and maintain the billing infrastructure that enables Zocdoc to collect payments from providers reliably and transparently. Our systems handle invoicing, payment processing, and financial reporting for thousands of healthcare practices.

Key Objectives

  • Ensure reliable and accurate billing for all provider accounts
  • Modernize payment infrastructure with Stripe Payment Intents
  • Support new account segments (mid-market) with appropriate billing models
  • Improve self-service billing capabilities for providers
