Provider Onboarding (Peacock)

Making provider onboarding fast, simple, and delightful

Jira Board Confluence PagerDuty #provider-peacock-team

Team Mission

We enable healthcare providers to join Zocdoc quickly and easily, ensuring they can start seeing patients as soon as possible. Our focus is on reducing friction in the onboarding process while maintaining data quality and compliance.

Key Objectives

  • Reduce time-to-activation for new providers
  • Increase self-service onboarding completion rates
  • Improve provider data quality through better validation
  • Scale onboarding infrastructure for enterprise customers
